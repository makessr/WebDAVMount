WebDAVMount development preview

Bundled rclone: https://github.com/rclone/rclone
This package uses a custom v1.76.0 source build excluding SMB registration and
guarding delayed mount cleanup by mount-instance identity. The writeback2
overlay adds conditional WebDAV writeback, and its policy inputs are bundled.
The custom-build notice and upstream MIT text are preserved in RCLONE-README.txt.
Exact source, patch and build provenance are recorded in engine-build/.

This desktop program includes Wails, go-webview2, golang.org/x/sys and other
Go dependencies, and the Vue and Wails browser runtimes. Dependency license
and notice files from the build inputs are preserved under licenses/.

WinFsp and Microsoft WebView2 are external runtime dependencies, not bundled
or installed by this package. Their installers and applicable terms are
provided by their respective projects.

WebDAVMount's own code is licensed under Apache-2.0. See
licenses/WebDAVMount/LICENSE and NOTICE. Third-party components retain their
respective licenses and notices.

Scope of the current notice collection:
Root license/notice files for desktop Go build inputs are copied. New builds
record frontend bundle modules and retain their package notices, Vue's umbrella
license, and Vite/Rolldown generated-code notices including third-party texts.
The frontend inventory is bound to the executable and bundle hashes in
licenses/frontend-index/NOTICE.json. Historical builds without that receipt
retain the conservative production-dependency notice set. This
does not establish that every nested/vendor notice has been collected.
The selected custom rclone executable contains its own embedded dependencies.
Exact-version module root and nested notice files are collected under
licenses/rclone_*/ with their provenance and hashes in
licenses/rclone-index/NOTICE.json. Packaging verifies the engine hash,
embedded dependency module checksums, inventory and copied notice hashes.
These collected texts still require review of terms and attribution
obligations; preserving them is not a complete legal-compliance review.

Source-access notices and exact-version links for four MPL dependencies
are provided at
licenses/rclone-source-access/NOTICE.txt. A local companion source artifact
includes the exact engine source ZIP and rebuild inputs. Other redistribution
and attribution clearance remains pending; historical official-engine packages
retain their earlier unresolved license findings.
